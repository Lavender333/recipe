Velvet Oat
Blueprint

13 Flavors · Honey Edition

3 Jars per Batch 6h Chill No Refined Sugar

Created by Antoinette Williams

How It Works

4 Steps.
That's It.

1
Jars First
Add ⅓ cup dry rolled oats into each of 3 airtight jars.
2
Blend
Plant milk + silken tofu + sweetener + nut butter → blend HIGH, 45 seconds.
3
Combine
Pour evenly into the 3 jars. Stir well.
4
Refrigerate
Seal lids. Chill at least 6 hours. Wake up to velvet pudding.
No dates? Use honey.
Quick swap — same macros, same texture
1
Swap 4 dates → 2–3 tbsp raw honey (use 2 tbsp for lighter sweet, 3 tbsp for full sweet)
2
Reduce plant milk from 1¼ cups → 1 cup
3
Calories & carbs stay virtually the same

Stock These

Your Pantry
Essentials

🌾
Rolled Oats
Old-fashioned only. Not instant.
🥛
Plant Milk
Soy or Yellow Pea — 8g protein per cup, unflavored.
🧊
Silken Tofu
Shelf-stable box from the global foods aisle. Blends silky smooth.
🌴
Sweetener
Medjool dates or raw honey. Both work — see the swap rule above.
🥜
Nut Butter
Peanut, almond, or cashew. Ingredients should be nuts + salt only.
